Free and Pro

The free app is the real app, with ads. Symptom Diary Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play; the price shows in the app in your currency.

Free

  • Unlimited episodes and quick logging
  • Basic charts
  • Health Connect sleep and steps
  • Ads on history screens

Pro

  • No ads
  • Trigger correlation and medication effectiveness
  • Doctor-ready PDF export
  • IBS / low-FODMAP mode
  • Optional encrypted cloud backup (when available)

Private by design

Health data is sensitive. Your diary stays on your phone; Health Connect data is read only with your permission and only on the device. Nothing is shared unless you export a PDF or opt into backup.

Read the full privacy policy · How to delete your data

In your language

On first launch you pick your language. The app downloads a free on-device translation pack from Google (ML Kit) for it and translates its screens on your phone; you can change the language any time in Settings. Translations are automatic, so a phrase may read a little differently than a native speaker would put it.

FAQ

Is my health data uploaded?

No. Episodes, triggers and Health Connect data stay on your phone. The only way data leaves is a PDF you export or the optional cloud backup if you turn it on.

What does it read from Health Connect?

Sleep sessions and steps, with your permission, to correlate with episodes. You can revoke access in Health Connect at any time.

Is it medical advice?

No. It is a diary and statistics tool, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or treat anything.

Does it support IBS?

Yes: an IBS / low-FODMAP mode (Pro) adds food logging and a reference list.

How do I get help?

Open an issue on our GitHub page; please never post health details there.
